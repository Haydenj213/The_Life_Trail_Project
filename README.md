# "The Life Trail"
## Group 35 - Senior Design 2026-2027

**Descriptions**
The Life Trail is a web game that lets young people experience the impact of their financial 
decisions throughout the course of their journey, inspired by The Oregon Trail, which is an 
educational game that teaches players about hardships and decision-making. Since finance is 
such an “intimidating” topic to young adults, we hope this project can assist and help them 
understand what they can be more in control of their life and understand the impact of their 
financial decisions.
